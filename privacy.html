Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how this internal automation tool accesses and processes data through Google APIs.

What data we access

The tool accesses email messages in designated mailboxes to identify and extract information relevant to internal business record-keeping.

How data is used

Extracted information is used solely to update internal spreadsheets and records for accounting and operational purposes. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to internal business operations.

Data sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties, except service providers strictly necessary to operate the tool (such as data processing infrastructure), under standard confidentiality terms.

Data retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for internal business and record-keeping purposes, and is not retained longer than required.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the tool administrator.
